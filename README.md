An AI agent found the wildest half-second in Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1 — an alligator climbing out of a Vice City swimming pool while an animal-control officer hoses it down — and cut it into a 16-second vertical Short: the moment in slow motion under a hook headline, a full-speed replay, then an on-screen pitch telling viewers they can make money on make.fun by making clips with AI. The video goes up public on YouTube, its address lands in VIDEO.json, and its views decide the entry's place. See CLIP.md for the moment, the creative choices and how the cut was rendered.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
