# The clip

**Source:** Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1, Rockstar Games' official YouTube channel
(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QdBZY2fkU-0), downloaded with yt-dlp in the build session.
The other official trailers (Trailer 2 and the Launch Trailer) were reviewed the same way;
this moment beat them.

**The moment (trailer time 42.4–43.9 s):** an in-world social post by "OfficialPOACH":
an animal-control officer hoses down an alligator that is climbing out of a backyard
swimming pool in Vice City, under the caption "Stay Alert VC! Remember to check for
uninvited guests before the pool party."

**Why this one:** it is readable in under a second with the sound off — man, hose,
alligator, swimming pool — it is funny without context, it is bright daylight with no
weapons or gore, and "an uninvited guest in the pool" is the most shareable beat in the
trailer. One moment, not a montage, as the bounty asks.

**The cut (16.6 s, 1080×1920, 30 fps), all rendered with ffmpeg in the build session:**

1. **Hook (0–3.0 s):** the moment at 0.5× slow motion, tight vertical crop on the
   alligator (crop x=620 of the 1920-wide frame), headline burned in:
   "BUAYA NYASAR / KE KOLAM RENANG! / cuma di GTA 6..." (Indonesian — the channel's
   audience — for "AN ALLIGATOR STRAYED INTO A SWIMMING POOL! only in GTA 6...").
2. **Replay (3.0–5.2 s):** the same beat at full speed, blur-fill wide shot so the
   officer and the pool read too, caption "Tamu tak diundang di Vice City"
   ("An uninvited guest in Vice City").
3. **Pitch (5.2–16.6 s):** the shot looped slow and darkened under four text beats,
   each held 2–3 s inside the safe area: "Klip ini dipotong / oleh AI agent"
   ("This clip was cut by an AI agent"), "Kamu juga bisa dibayar / bikin klip pakai AI"
   ("You can get paid too, making clips with AI"), "Gas, cek di sini /
   make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01", and a closer "make.fun / bikin klip pakai AI,
   dibayar beneran". The trailer's own music runs under the whole clip.
